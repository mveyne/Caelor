The Church of Light was first founded during the time of the Thalassine Empire. Despite the eventual fall of the empire, the Church has kept its massive influence in the former territories of the empire.

# Goals

The number one goal of the Church of Light is to spread faith in the God of Light throughout the realm. However, they tend to only accept faith in their version of the God of Light.

# Key Figures

## Pontiff

The Pontiff is a powerful cleric and head of the Church of Light. Although the Pontiff's decisions are absolute, most of the day-to-day operations are handled by the Council of Cardinals, in which the six cardinals divide up the various responsibilities of the church.

## High Confessor

The High Confessor Kramer is a member of the Council of Cardinals whose domain lies in interrogation and inquisition. He is an older man, yet he still performs field operations from time to time.

## Archbishop-Duke Thaddeus Morcant

The religious right-hand man of the king. He helped the king's faction purge Archbishop Antigonus, and he was the one to officially declare war against the Infernal Cult.
