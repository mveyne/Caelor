The Ashewyn Dynasty emerged as the rulers of Caelor after a brutal civil war over 800 years ago. With the help of their allies, including various noble families and the Church of Light, King Emmanuel Ashewyn defeated the brutal and corrupt Mad King of the Corveth Dynasty. 

The dynasty first followed a feudal system where the various nobles and important religious figures, centered around the Twelve Peers. Each party played a particular role in the government of the kingdom. The nobility acted as counselors and agents for the king - he was supposed to consult them regularly, to only make decisions with their approval. They kept order in their spheres of influence, went on embassies for the king, ran law courts and collected taxes, and supplied the bulk of the royal army. The king, meanwhile, was vital for the aristocracy - he provided an arbitrator for their disputes, provided patronage for them and for their followers (in particular for church offices) and, most importantly of all, legitimized the power they wielded and the actions they performed using that power.

Over the last few centuries, the power has increasingly consolidated into the hands of the Ashewyn family. The military, law, and church is now under the direct control of the king.

# Goals

## Crucible

Though unsure of Princess Althaea’s goal, the party helped her recover a crucible from the ruins of Valreth. Created by a Thalassine scholar named Vitruvius, this crucible seems to be able to compress and store large amounts of energy within.

# Key Figures

## King Emmanuel

King Emmanuel was the founding king of the Ashewyn dynasty. He is described as a gallant figure who overthrew the fiendish members of the Corveth Dynasty and their underlings, the Infernal Cult.

Within the ruins of Valreth, the party discovered that his original name was Rivalen. During the reign of the Corveth Dynasty, Rivalen led a successful rebellion against the corrupt monarchy.

## The Bulwark King

Approximately a century ago, the nation of Flaneas launched a full-scale invasion of Caelor. Outmatched and unable to coordinate their disparate noble-led armies, Caelor’s defenses collapsed, allowing Flanean forces to advance deep into the country and threaten the capital itself. In response to this crisis, the king assumed direct command of the fractured armies, unifying them under a single centralized force. Through this consolidation and his keen military insight, the Bulwark King Valen Ashewyn succeeded in driving back the invaders and reclaiming the occupied territories.

Following the war’s conclusion, the Bulwark King refused to return military authority to the nobles. Instead, he established a permanent central army and outlawed the formation of private forces, including those raised by noble houses.

He died at the Battle of the Shattered Crown at the hands of [[Infernal Cult#Emberlord Severik|Severik the Ashen-Hand]] around 90 years ago.

## The Scholar King

Little is publicly known about the Scholar King, Valara. She is best remembered for her successful defense of the realm against the Infernal Cult and the establishment of a unified legal framework enforced upon peasant and noble alike. She is also known as an extraordinary wizard, effective both in and out of battle, and an avid scholar on the subject of the Thalassine Empire.

## The Splendor King

The Splendor King, Charles, is remembered as both a great patron of the arts and an extraordinary artist in his own right. His push to establish a new cultural center for Caelor led to the creation of the city of Brightcourt, a city of splendor considered by some as the new capital of the country.

## The Sun King

The Sun King, Lorian, is the current ruler of the Ashewyn Dynasty. He succeeded his father  a decade ago. Few know his real appearance, as he only ever shows himself in public with a hooded robe and mask. 

# Relationships

## Nobles

**Lothaine**: Centered around House Lothaine, they are the largest group of nobles that oppose the king.
**Rosarium**: A neutral group of nobles. The party does not know much about them.
**Lioness**: She hates the Crown for having killed her husband. Seven served on her crew for six years.

## Royal Knights

The Royal Knights serve at the behest of the king. They are to protect him even if it costs them their lives.

Although royal knights are loyal to the crown, some seem to be loyal to Princess Althaea while others are loyal to the king himself.

## Umbral Hand

The leader of the Umbral Hand is the Royal Princess, Althaea. This group consists of assassins trained in infiltration, surveillance, and assassination.

Beyond the ordinary members of the organization, the party has come across a man in blackened armor and a robed man with gauze along the arms. The party has also met a man named Lorenzo, who seemed to have gained a certain amount of trust from Althaea.

The Umbral Hand maintains a loose set of associates. One of these associates is Lady Mira Borvar, daugher of Countess Borvar, who was tasked by Althaea to recover an artifact deep within Velreth’s underground. 

## Church

The party does not know much about the relation between the church and the king. The king seems to have gained spiritual leadership within Caelor, but the exact changes this has caused within local chapters of the church is unclear.
