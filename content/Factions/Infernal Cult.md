# Goals

## Destroying the Ashewyn Dynasty

After the great betrayal 750 years ago, the cult has dedicated itself to a great Judgement: the destruction of the Ashewyn Dynasty and any who stand in their way of that goal. Though their hatred for the people of Caelor have dimmed over time, the ancestral spirits still crave vengeance for their wrongful deaths.

The cult has attempted to invade Caelor eight separate times. Though largely unsuccessful, the eighth attempt led to near victory under the leadership of Emberlord Severik.

# Key Figures

## Severik the Ashen-Hand

After the successful defense of Caelor against Flaneas, the Infernal Cult took advantage of the nation's weakness to launch an invasion. At its head was Severik. Severik quickly advanced through Caelor and threatened the capital. In a massive confrontation with the Bulwark King, Severik slayed him in single combat, gaining the title Severik the Ashen-Hand.
