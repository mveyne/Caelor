# Background
To the east of the World Tree, cupping the Great Sea from the north, lies the nation of Flaneas. Heavily influenced by the elves, this country of half-elves and humans are a historical rival to Caelor. Although not the most heavily armed Many privateers carry the symbol of Flaneas upon their flag, a green tree with a great many branches.
