## Corveth Dynasty

Before the Ashewyn took the crown, Caelor was ruled by House Corveth. Their god was the Abyssal Serpent. This god of sacrifice seemed to have brought the worst out of House Corveth. The strong consumed the weak. The ruler consumed the ruled. The faithful consumed themselves so that the faith may grow fatter. Corveth lords held court atop altars. Their judges passed sentence in chambers built around sacrifical pits. Chidlren were tithed. Whole villages were "offered" years of poor harvest, as though the soil grew lean because of the Serpent's hunger rather than because the lords had taken too much.

The Corveth Dynasty ruled Caelor between year 319–393. Their reign was put to an end by Rivalen, whose Ashewyn Dynasty rules to this day.
