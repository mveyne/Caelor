Known as the Golden Lion of the North, House Lothaine is one of the Twelve Peers and the main opposition to the Ashewyn lineage.

# Key Figures

## Cyrus Lothaine

As one of the most powerful nobles in Caelor, Cyrus has a large amount of influence in many fields. Their largest specialty is the extraction and processing of war magical minerals and metals.

He has shown himself to be a powerful sorcerer.

## Dimitri Lothaine

Lord Dmitri is 25–30 in age with well groomed, short blond hair and blue eyes. He wears a royal blue velvet doublet, accented by intricate silver embroidery along the hem and cuffs. On his chest is emblazoned the symbol of the Golden Lion, rendered in gold thread. His heavy blue mantle is drapped over his right shoulder like a half cloak, fastened with a silver brooch and leaving his sword arm unobstructed.

As the successor the House Lothaine, Dimitri is a skilled warrior and well-versed in ice magic.

## House Ferrmont

Count Edmund wears a high collared, dark grey doublet made of silk weave and filled with intricate damask in the form of geometric patterns. Although not yet old, he does look to be middle aged in his very late 30's or early 40's with black hair. He is a tall man, with a cold and serious look and short, neat black hair framing his face. He has a well-groomed goatee. 

He makes much of his income on the mining of mundane metals, though he does have alternative sources of income.

He is married to Countess Lydia. She has plain brown hair and subtle accessories in her hair and ears. She is wearing a red dress with golden embroidery in various patterns. She is wearing a green half mask and has striking green eyes.
