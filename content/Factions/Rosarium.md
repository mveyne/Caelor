The Rosarium was a somewhat informal gathering of peers with common origins and history. They all tend to have flower-themed coat of arms. They became a more formal alliance in recent years after their power began getting stripped away. They are mostly concentrated in the northeastern section of Caelor, though they have friends in many locations.

# Figures
## Viscount Lucian Velerian
Lucian was a viscount of the Rosarium and vassal of Count Belmont. The symbol of his house, the star-lily, is a relatively rare magical flower that glows brilliant purple in the moonlight. The star-lily was a flower loved by the first lady of House Velerian; it became for those in House Velerian to offer a star-lily to their betrothed.

Lucian was known as an eccentric figure who loved to hear stories from adventurers and wandering knights. After the death of his family nearly a decade prior, Lucian Velerian hired a knight named Sir Elric. After years of faithful service, Sir Elric revealed the loyalty to his true master, the crown. This betrayal led to the death of Lucian at the hands of his knight and a powerful new magical cannon.

## Count Belmont
He seems to be the head of the house that House Velerian was under. The token of the star-lily serves as a piece of political currency. As the superior to the fallen Viscount, Count Belmont is honor bound to repay the debt.
