After the dissolution of the Thalassine Empire, the country of Thal was left in its wake. As the birthplace of the [[Church of Light]] and a previously massive empire, the people of Thal are proud people--some so proud that they strive for the reestablishment of the Empire.

## Geography

Jutting up into the Nostrum Sea from the south, much of the territory is protected by an imposing southern mountain range with few passages through. Mount Caelum is sometimes called the Holy Mount.

The capital of Thal is Thalus, the birthplace of both the [[Thalassine Empire]] and the [[Church of Light]]. As both an important cultural and religious center of the continent, the beautiful city of white stone and marble contains imposing buildings of administration and religion.

## Key Figures

### Senator Titus

During the war between Thal and the Seyrathi Dominion, Titus and Lorick became fast friends. Thanks to their many contributions during this war, Lorick joined Church of Light as a paladin, and Titus became a senator of Thal.

## Goals

### Reestablishing the Empire

The people of Thal long to become an empire once more. In an attempt to set the stage for invasion, Senator Titus has sent Lorick to figure out the weakness of Caelor and make connections with possible assets.
