# Culture & People

Seyrathi has a majority population of dragonborn, who have historically held control over power within the Dominion. This has led to discrimination and even slavery against other races, especially Orcs.

The people of the Seyrathi Dominion believe in the Goddess of the Moon. They believe Goddess of the Moon went to sleep after her injury at the hands of the God of Light and will wake up again once the Seyrathi Dominion becomes an empire once more. 

This belief has led to a monotheistic theocracy, in which the Moon Goddess’s apostle has substantial influence over the affairs of the Dominion.

# Current State

After regaining their strength over the last millennia, the Seyrathi Dominion waged war against the remnants of the Thalassine Empire, Thal. Though peace was struck after a few years of conflict, the Seyrathi Dominion is considered to have been on the losing side of the conflict. This loss has demoralized the people of the Dominion and reenergized the people of Thal.

# History

A millennia ago, the influence of Nulfaga, Apostle of the Moon Goddess, enabled this dragonborn nation to become the dominant power in the continent. This dominance came to an end with their defeat at the hands of the Thalassine Empire, whose Emperor of Light is said to have slayed Nulfaga and turned Thal from a small country to a proper empire.
