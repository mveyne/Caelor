Centuries after the fall of the Thalassine Empire, the country of Caelor--a previous vassal state of the empire--entered an age of feudal society under the dynasty of House Ashewyn. However, in the last two centuries, the kings have been slowly centralizing their power. Although recent years have been calm, an underlying tension permeates the country. The nobles have been attempting to accrue power, the church has divided, and the Infernal Cult is preparing to strike the lands once more.
