Centuries after the fall of the Thalassine Empire, the country of Caelor--a previous vassal state of the empire--has entered an age of feudal society under the dynasty of House Ashewyn. However, in the last century, the kings have been slowly centralizing their power. Although recent years have been calm, there is an underlying tension. The nobles have been attempting to accrue power, and the demonic cult is preparing to strike the lands once more.
